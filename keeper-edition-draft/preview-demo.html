For grown-ups · Wi-Fi & help

Teach your dragon home Wi-Fi

Internet is optional. You can always play by touch or while connected directly to your dragon.

Choose a 2.4 GHz network. If your Wi-Fi name doesn't appear, type it yourself. Leave the password blank only for an open network.

Moving house? (The Purple Nose-Hold)

Hold the dragon's touch pad while plugging it in. Keep holding through the five blue eye-blinks, then release when its scales turn purple. Join the dragon network using your setup card and open 192.168.4.1.

If your phone says the dragon network has no internet, choose to stay connected. This keeps your creations and your dragon's setup-network password (dragon123).
