# Dragon Kingdom

User manual

For a young dragon keeper and their grown-ups

## Welcome to your dragon

Choose a dragon, give it some magic, or create one of your own. You can play
with the touch pad on the dragon or use a phone browser. No app or coding is
needed. Dragon Kingdom is designed for one child. Ask a grown-up to help with
power and the first phone connection.

## Start playing

1. Ask a grown-up to connect the power supply supplied or specified by your maker.
2. Wait for startup to finish. Blue pulses are normal while the dragon starts.
3. Tap the touch pad to choose the next dragon.
4. Double tap to put your dragon to sleep or wake it.
5. Hold the pad to change its glow. Release, then hold again to change direction.

## Your three touch moves

- **Tap:** next dragon, or wake from sleep.
- **Double tap:** sleep or wake.
- **Hold:** adjust glow in small steps.

Touch play works without a phone or internet. Start with **All** to explore
every dragon. Optional profiles let you keep favourite collections. An empty
profile needs favourites or a creation before there is a dragon to play with.

**Keep your setup card.** It has your dragon's Wi-Fi name. The dragon's setup
password is **dragon123** (all lowercase).

# Connect a phone

First setup - a grown-up's job

## Open Dragon Kingdom

1. Power the dragon and wait for startup to finish.
2. Open the phone's Wi-Fi settings. Join the dragon network named on its setup card.
3. Enter **dragon123** (all lowercase).
4. If the phone says this network has no internet, choose to stay connected.
5. Open a browser and type **http://192.168.4.1** in the address bar.
6. When asked to log in, use **parent** with password **dragon123**.

The page may open automatically. If it does not, use the address above.
You can play while connected directly to the dragon. Home Wi-Fi is optional.
The updated firmware replaces older random setup passwords; older cards may
show the previous password. Your home Wi-Fi keeps its own password.

## Optional: connect to home Wi-Fi

1. Open **For grown-ups · Wi-Fi & help**.
2. Tap **Find my Wi-Fi**, then choose your network in the Wi-Fi name box.
3. Enter its password. Use **Show password** if you want to check your typing.
4. Tap **Connect my dragon** and wait for the result.
5. After success, the full bookmark address appears above the play controls.
6. Rejoin home Wi-Fi on the phone. Open that address and bookmark it.

Choose a **2.4 GHz** home network. If it is not listed, type its name yourself.
If the password is incorrect, check it and try again.

Bookmark the **.local** name shown on your page, such as
**http://Dragon-Kingdom-a1b2c3.local/** (example only). Each dragon has its own name.
It stays the same if the router changes the numeric address; no router setup
is needed. The page also shows a numeric address as a fallback.

# Wake with a sunrise

A gentle daily light alarm

## Set your wake-up time

1. Open **Sunrise alarm** on the phone page.
2. Tick **Wake me every day**.
3. Choose your **Wake-up time** and **Sunrise length (minutes)**.
4. Tap **Save alarm**. Check that the daily sunrise is saved.

The whole wing area, including the lowest wing pixels and wing edges, brightens from deep orange to warm sunrise
light. The sunrise starts before your chosen wake-up time. For example,
**7:00** with a **20-minute** sunrise starts at **6:40**.

It works while the dragon is asleep. After wake-up time, the wings hold their
glow for **30 minutes**, then return to the previous lighting. Available power
still limits brightness. This is a light alarm; it does not make a sound.

## Try it or stop it

- **Try a 30-second sunrise** previews the effect, then returns after a short hold.
- **Stop sunrise** stops the current effect without disabling tomorrow's alarm.
- A touch or play control also stops the current sunrise.
- To disable the daily alarm, untick **Wake me every day** and tap **Save alarm**.

## Keep its clock ready

Keep the dragon powered. Saving uses your phone's clock and daylight-saving
rules. The schedule is kept when power is disconnected, but the clock needs
internet time or this page opened again after a power cut. Without either,
the alarm waits until the clock is set.

Wait for the saved alarm to load before editing it. If loading fails, the page
tries again. **Set clock from my phone** can also recover the saved fields;
check the time, sunrise length and checkbox before tapping **Save alarm**.

Open and save the alarm at least once a year, and after moving to a different
time zone. This refreshes its daylight-saving rules for future restarts.

# Choose your collection

Profiles and favourites

## All or a personal profile

You can keep using **All** without making a profile. Optional profiles hold
different favourite collections for the same child.

Use **My profile** to switch collections. **All** contains every supplied
dragon and every saved creation. It cannot be renamed or deleted.

A named profile contains its saved favourites plus creations made in that
profile. **My dragon** shows only that collection. The first item is selected
when the dragon starts, and single taps cycle through the same collection.

## Make a profile

1. Open **My favourites & starting glow**.
2. Enter a **New profile name** and tap **Add profile**.
3. Tick the dragons you want as favourites. You can choose up to **12**.
4. Choose the **Starting glow**, then tap **Save profile**.

Favourites use the order in which you tick them. Untick and tick one again to
move it to the end. Your own creations appear after the other favourites.
A new empty profile says **Add favourites or make a dragon**. Play controls
stay unavailable until you add something to that collection.

There are **three named profile spaces**, plus All. Profiles and saved creations
are kept when power is disconnected. Temporary play effects are not saved as
the profile's startup settings.

## Delete a profile

Select the named profile, open its settings, and tap **Delete this profile**.
Read the confirmation before agreeing. Its custom dragons move into **All**,
and the profile space becomes available again. All is protected.

# Make your own dragon

Colours, parts and magic

## Create and save

1. Tap **Make a dragon** and choose **Start from**.
2. Enter a **Dragon name**.
3. Tap a part in the picture, or choose it from **Dragon part**.
4. Choose its **Colour** and **Magic on this part**.
5. Repeat for other parts, then tap **Save my dragon**.

Change the face, body, tail, horns, wing edges and wing interiors separately.
All other pixels belong to a wing. Untouched parts keep their colours and
patterns. **Use original colour for this part** restores that part's base colours.

The picture is a still preview of the 64 lights. Save to see the real result;
room light and available power can make its glow look different.

## Edit, copy or cancel

- Select your creation in **My dragon**, then tap **Make a dragon** to edit it.
- **Save my dragon** replaces a creation you own.
- **Save a new copy** keeps your earlier creation as well.
- Editing a supplied dragon, or a creation from another collection, makes a copy.
- **Cancel** discards the draft without changing the saved dragon.

There are **eight custom dragon spaces** across all collections. When full,
edit or delete a creation you own. Deleting it also removes it from favourites.
Supplied dragons stay.

## Play some magic

On the play screen, try **Glitter**, **Fire**, **Breathe** or **Rainbow**. An
effect continues until you change it. Tap **Back to my dragon** to restore its
normal colours and region effects. **Glow** changes brightness; the sleep
button changes to **Wake my dragon** when the dragon is asleep.

Wait for each change to finish before the next action. See **Help with play
and saves** if controls are unavailable.

# Reconnect and get help

Changing Wi-Fi or solving a problem

## Reopen setup

1. Disconnect power from the dragon.
2. Hold the touch pad while reconnecting power.
3. Keep holding through the blue startup pulses.
4. Release when the purple setup indication appears after startup.
5. Join the dragon network with **dragon123** and open **http://192.168.4.1**.

This keeps the saved home Wi-Fi details. To remove them, use **Forget home
Wi-Fi** in the grown-ups area and confirm. Then rejoin the dragon network.
Your creations and setup-network password are kept.

## If the phone cannot connect

- **The phone says "no internet".** This is normal on the dragon's own network.
  Stay connected and open the setup address in a browser.
- **The page stops after home Wi-Fi setup.** Rejoin home Wi-Fi on the phone.
  Use the address shown in the setup tab or your saved bookmark.
- **The .local bookmark will not open.** Use the same home Wi-Fi as the dragon
  and try its numeric address. Guest or isolated networks can block both.
  Use a network that allows local devices, or reopen direct setup.
- **The dragon network never appears.** Ask the maker to check the specified
  power supply and cable. A low-power startup can keep phone Wi-Fi off while
  touch play still works. Do not add another power source.
- **The setup card is missing.** Look for **Dragon-Kingdom-XXXXXX** in Wi-Fi settings
  and use **dragon123**. If older firmware rejects it, ask the maker for help.

# Help with play and saves

A grown-up can help you check these

## When a control is unavailable

- **The page says "Please wait for your dragon".** Let the current action finish.
  Controls become available again when the result arrives or an error is shown.
- **A profile has no dragons.** Add favourites and save, make a dragon, or select
  All. An empty profile does not show the full catalog automatically.
- **The dragon is dark or dim.** Check Sleep and Glow. It dims in a dark room
  and stays lit in daylight. Allow a few seconds for changes to settle.
- **Repeated red flashes or restarting.** Ask a grown-up to disconnect power
  and contact the maker. Do not open the product or change its wiring.

## If a save or connection fails

A failed save leaves the editor open so you can keep your changes. If the page
says the collection changed, make a note of your unsaved name and colour
choices before reloading; reloading discards the draft. Check what is already
saved before trying again, especially after the connection dropped during a save.

After a firmware update, reload the phone page before making changes. An old
page may ask you to reload instead of accepting a save.

Touch play continues when home Wi-Fi is unavailable. The dragon retries its
saved network. Use the power-on setup hold if you need a direct phone connection.

## If the alarm needs attention

Wait for the alarm fields to load. If they do not recover, use **Set clock from
my phone** or reopen the page. Check all three settings before saving. After a
power cut, leave the dragon connected to home Wi-Fi for internet time, or open
the page to set its clock from the phone.

# Keep this page handy

Care, reminders and your device details

## A grown-up connects the power

Use the power supply supplied or specified by the maker. Keep the dragon dry
and handle its cable gently. Ask a grown-up for help if a connector or cable
is damaged. Do not open the enclosure or connect extra power sources.

For a prototype connected to a computer: **laptop USB and external/main power
must not be connected at the same time**. The maker should handle any computer
testing or wiring changes.

## Remember

- No app or coding is required for normal use.
- Touch play does not need internet.
- Keep the setup card and bookmark your dragon's .local phone-control address.
- All is the complete collection; named profiles are personal collections.
- Save your profile or creation before disconnecting power.
- Glow is adjusted for room light and available power. A 100% setting does not
  override those adjustments.
