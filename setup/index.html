Welcome, dragon keeper

Touch play needs no phone, app or account. A grown-up can use a phone browser for colours, collections, Wi-Fi and updates.

Touch commands

One tap: next dragon or wake. Two quick taps: sleep/wake. Hold: adjust glow; release and hold again to change direction.

First phone connection

  1. Read this page while your phone has internet. Power one lamp from its normal bottom USB-C supply and wait for startup.
  2. In phone Wi-Fi settings choose Dragon-Kingdom-XXXXXX, using the name of your own lamp. The password is dragon123 (lowercase).
  3. Choose Stay connected if the phone says there is no internet. Open http://192.168.4.1/ in your browser. That address works only while joined to the lamp's setup Wi-Fi.
  4. Play without logging in. Open Parent settings and unlock with dragon123 for Wi-Fi and updates.

Connect your home Wi-Fi

  1. In Parent settings choose your 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi and enter its private password.
  2. Wait for the result. Note the lamp's home address, switch your phone back to home Wi-Fi, and bookmark that address in the normal browser.
  3. Setup Wi-Fi closes shortly after a successful home connection. The computer updater finds lamps on the same home network.

To reopen setup, disconnect and reconnect the normal product power. Saved Wi-Fi and dragons are retained. No touch hold or factory reset is needed.

Updates showing an HTTPS error? Read the update guide. Our recommended Windows/Mac Easy Update app supplies the repair over local Wi-Fi; normal touch and local page controls remain available.

Power and help

Use the supply provided or specified by the maker. The bottom USB-C connector is power-only. Never connect laptop USB and product power together. Ordinary Wi-Fi updates do not require opening the box.

If the lamp keeps restarting or flashing red, disconnect its power and contact the maker. Do not factory-reset it to repair a power problem.
