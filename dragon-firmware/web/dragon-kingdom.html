Sunrise alarm

Wake up with your dragon. Its wings slowly glow from deep orange to warm sunrise light.

The sunrise starts this many minutes before your wake-up time. It works while the dragon is asleep and stays bright for 30 minutes afterwards, then returns to your previous lighting.

Loading alarm…

Keep the dragon powered for its alarm. Saving uses your phone's time and daylight-saving rules. After a power cut, it needs internet time or this page opened again. A touch or play control stops the current sunrise.
For grown-ups · Wi-Fi & help

Connect to home Wi-Fi

Internet is optional. You can play while connected directly to your dragon.

Choose a 2.4 GHz network. If your Wi-Fi name doesn't appear, type it yourself. Leave the password blank only for an open network.

Need to reconnect?

Hold the dragon's touch pad while plugging it in. Keep holding through the blue startup pulses, then release when you see purple. Join the dragon network using your setup card and open 192.168.4.1.

If your phone says the dragon network has no internet, choose to stay connected. This keeps your dragon's setup-network password.
