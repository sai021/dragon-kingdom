Teach your dragon home Wi-Fi

Internet is optional. You can always play by touch or while connected directly to your dragon.

Choose a 2.4 GHz network. If your Wi-Fi name doesn't appear, type it yourself. Leave the password blank only for an open network.

Open setup Wi-Fi again

Power cycle your dragon. Its password-protected network opens at every startup. Join it using your setup card and open 192.168.4.1, or scan the local-controls QR on the card. Your phone may offer a sign-in page; automatic opening is not guaranteed. Choose to stay connected if it says there is no internet.

Saved home Wi-Fi is tried for up to 60 seconds. Failure keeps setup Wi-Fi open until you retry. Success closes setup Wi-Fi after 15 seconds, even if your phone is still connected. Join your home Wi-Fi and reopen the home address shown above. A later home-network drop requires a power cycle to reopen setup. This keeps your creations and your dragon's setup-network password (dragon123).

Backup & restore

Backups include collections, creations, sunrise, rotation and light calibration. Wi-Fi and parent passwords are excluded.

Firmware update

Checking your board type…

On home Wi-Fi with internet, a parent can check GitHub and approve installation. Your dragon verifies the signed release and downloads it directly. It never installs updates automatically.

Direct updates need the dragon connected to home Wi-Fi with internet. Its setup network has no internet.

Beta firmware is experimental. Choose it only if you are comfortable recovering your ESP32 through its internal USB connector. Have the recovery instructions and a computer ready.

Online updates need home Wi-Fi and a synced clock. File upload works offline.

For an offline update: first use home Wi-Fi or mobile data to download the matching signed .dragon.json package from GitHub releases on your phone or PC. Then join the dragon network again, reopen this page, unlock Parent settings and select the saved file below. Uploading that file needs no internet. Keep power connected throughout the update.

USB recovery: disconnect the bottom product USB-C power cable before connecting laptop USB. Remove one screw to access the internal ESP32 USB. The bottom USB-C port is power-only. Never connect laptop USB and external/main power simultaneously.
