Keep your dragon up to date

A computer can deliver a signed update over your home Wi-Fi. No USB cable to the lamp and no opening the enclosure.

Windows and Mac downloads are being prepared. The revised manual is available now. This page will show the download buttons when each build is ready. Your normal touch and local phone controls still work if the lamp reports an HTTPS update error.

How the computer updater works

  1. Keep the lamp on its normal product supply and connect the computer and lamp to the same home network. Set up home Wi-Fi first if needed.
  2. Download the updater for your computer from this page and open it. Allow access to your local network if the operating system asks.
  3. Choose Find dragons, select yours and enter the existing parent password (normally dragon123). If it is missing, choose Add address with the IP address from the lamp page.
  4. Review the installed and offered firmware, then choose Update selected dragon. The program saves a local backup and checks the signed file before uploading.
  5. Keep power, Wi-Fi and the app connected until its restart check finishes. Reopen the lamp page and check your saved dragons, touch and normal lighting.

The computer handles the internet download; the lamp only receives the update locally. The same app is an alternative for later compatible updates, with your click required each time.

Shipped beta-3 lamps

The updater will choose the old-format signed bridge automatically. Do not select a random raw .bin file or factory-reset the lamp to clear the HTTPS message.

Phone-only route

Board-matched repair downloads and exact file-upload instructions will appear here with the new release. Existing beta-9 downloads remain an experienced-tester prerelease; wait for the shipping release linked here.

Help finding your lamp

Use the normal home network, not a guest or isolated network. A VPN or firewall may prevent discovery. Follow your organisation's device rules; do not disable computer security. If the updater cannot reach the lamp, open its page directly or use the setup guide and contact the maker.
